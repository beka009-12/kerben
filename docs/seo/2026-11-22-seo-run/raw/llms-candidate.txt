# Kerben Travel

> Kerben Travel is a local tour operator in Bishkek: tours of Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. Mountains, lakes, nomadic culture and the Silk Road. Official website: https://www.kerben-travel.com (English, Russian, German, Chinese).

Contacts: phone and WhatsApp +996 705 120169; email kerbentravels@gmail.com; office in Bishkek, Mon–Sat, 11:00–20:00. Prices on the website are in US dollars; a booking is a request that a manager confirms before any payment.
